How to add your own song
------------------------
1. Open the admin page: /admin.html
2. Go to the Extras tab.
3. Under Music, click "Upload a song".
4. The song is saved here and automatically set as the opening track.
5. Click "Save changes".

On GitHub Pages (static mode) the admin downloads the song instead. Put that file in this
folder, then upload the updated content.js to your repository.

Supported: mp3, m4a, aac, wav, ogg, flac. Max 30 MB.
Keep songs you have the right to share.
